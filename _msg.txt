feat(viewer): auto-fill the sequence field from the loaded structure (MD/NMR/Docking)

When a structure (local file, PDB ID/URL or structure text) is loaded in the
shared 3D viewer and the page's sequence field is empty, the viewer now parses
the 1-letter sequence from the NGL structure (structure.getSequence) and the
page auto-fills activeTest.proteinSequence. This makes the per-atom table,
Per-Atom plots, secondary structure etc. work immediately after loading a PDB
without typing the sequence by hand.

- NMRMoleculeViewer: new onStructureSequence callback, invoked once per load.
- MDSections / NMRSections / DockingSections: fill proteinSequence when it is
  empty and the molecule type is a polymer (protein/dna/rna).
