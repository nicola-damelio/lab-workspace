fix(viewer): robust sequence auto-fill + load large structures with auto-hidden chains

1) Sequence auto-fill now works reliably: the viewer extracts the 1-letter
   sequence with a fallback chain (NGL getSequence(), then eachResidue with a
   3-letter→1-letter + nucleic map), so PDBs / .gro topologies that NGL does
   not auto-detect as polymers still populate the page's sequence field.

2) Large structure handling is no longer a blocking modal: the structure is
   loaded in full (topology/analysis keep everything) but only the FIRST CHAIN
   is rendered by default, with a light representation and no side-chains, so
   the browser does not freeze. A non-blocking amber banner warns the user that
   part of the molecule is hidden and offers "Show everything" (which re-adds
   the full representation on demand).
