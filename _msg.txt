feat(viewer): "Renumber from N" + 2 GB trajectory warning with auto stride

- Renumber panel: new "Renumber from [N]" input + button renumbers every
  residue consecutively starting from the user-chosen number, so the whole
  sequence is renumbered with a single click instead of editing residues one
  by one (defaults to 1; replaces the old fixed "Renumber from 1" button).
- Trajectory loading: files above 2 GB now get a warning that loading
  everything may exceed ~2 GB of memory. The suggested stride is recalculated
  so the effective loaded data stays under ~2 GB (based on the estimated
  bytes/frame), and the modal proposes that simplification or "Load
  everything" if the user declines. Files between 200 MB and 2 GB keep the
  previous ~2500-frame suggestion.
