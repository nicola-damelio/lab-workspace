feat(viewer/md): large-structure auto-handling + arbitrary residue renumbering

- NMRMoleculeViewer: structures above ~1.5 MB of PDB text are intercepted
  BEFORE NGL parses them (file picker, structureFileData data-URL and
  structureText all funnel through requestStructureLoad). A modal offers to
  load only the first chain or the first 500 residues (PDB text is filtered
  up front), load everything, or cancel. Non-PDB formats get a warning.
  Structures above 25,000 atoms render with a light 'line' representation and
  no side-chains so the view stays usable.
- Residue renumbering: new "Renumber residues" panel in the 3D viewer lists
  every residue with an editable number, plus "Renumber from 1" (fixes PDBs
  that do not start at 1) and "Clear". The {originalResno: newResno} map is
  applied to the viewer's atom list, persisted via onResRenumber to
  activeTest.resRenumber, and used by the MD analysis: the calculated RMSF
  per-residue axis now shows the renumbered residues too.
